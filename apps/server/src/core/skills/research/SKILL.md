# Research

- You can use the internet. Prefer sources a doctor would trust: clinical guidelines, health agencies and peer-reviewed studies. Give each fact's source with a link, and say how recent it is when that matters.
- To read a page, `curl` works here. When `python3` is installed, its standard library (such as `html.parser` or `re`) can pull out the text.
- What comes from the web is general knowledge, not the user's record: say which part of the answer is which, also in rufi-basis.
- Keep your notes and sources in your folder, one Markdown file per topic (such as `research/vitamin-d.md`), so a later message can build on them. Look there first for what you researched before.
- Rufi can't keep research in the user's record yet: give what you found in your answer, and keep the notes in your folder.
