# 0009 · Documents are read in the background by a Rufi sub-agent, one job at a time

**Date:** 10 Oct 2026, its models 11 Oct 2026 (ADR 0010), its retries 17 Oct 2026 · **Status:** accepted

A document is read by a background job, outside the chat, by a **Rufi sub-agent**: an AI thread that Rufi starts, binds and checks itself, not the agent's own sub-agents (those inherit the chat's access and settings). The job table (`jobs`, `core/documents/reading.ts`) is built once for every kind of background work; reading is its first kind. Jobs run one at a time, oldest first, as each document is added (the first run tells the person that documents go to the AI provider; nothing falls back to another service). Each job targets pages of its document (all of them first; a retry only the pages that failed), and each run of it is a new attempt. The reader (`server/reader.ts`) runs on the private runner (`agent/codex/subagent.ts`, ADR-level rules in `AGENTS.md`): a fresh ephemeral thread with Rufi's reading instructions in place of the agent's, no history, files, commands, network or other sub-agents, and exactly two tools bound by code to this person, document and attempt: `get_page` (the page image, the PDF's own text as extra help with exact characters, the page's current entry, the note and progress) and `save_page` (one page's entry and the note, in one transaction, only for a target page looked at since the last compaction). The work lives in the database, in the reading's **notebook** (an entry per page and one note), never in the thread, so a compaction or a crash loses nothing: after a restart the job is queued again and its next run continues with the targets that have no entry; if every target has an entry from a run not recorded as failed, code finishes without a reader. Each entry stores its attempt. Requeuing or failing a run marks that attempt's entries as failed in the same transaction, without changing their contents. They remain in the notebook for Retry, which must get a normally completed turn before publishing them. Code doesn't judge an entry's contents: the reader marks an item it isn't sure of (`unsure`, with why) and notes what needs the person (`issues`), such as a report that may be another person's; for that, its run message names the run's person (the display name only). Entries an older Rufi saved keep the checks it stored with them (`entry_checks`: "may be incomplete", or an item flagged unsure), and finishing a job still reads them; new entries have none. **Only a reader turn that completed normally finishes a job**: the run is closed to writes first, then one transaction writes the target pages' states (a page with no entry is "not looked at"), the draft items from this job's entries (version 1, the reader's fields kept), the document's kind and date when every page agrees, its version raised, the job done, and the chat's end line as a Rufi event, with a note for the AI only when something needs the person. A failed, interrupted or late turn keeps the entries and makes no drafts: a passing failure (a lost connection, a restart) gets one automatic retry; sign-in and usage limits make the job wait visibly; anything else fails it with its end line, and Retry continues it with a fresh deadline (5 minutes plus one a page, at most an hour). The reader's version (instructions, entry format, model, effort, page preparation, tools) and the AI use are stored with each job. Considered: code checks of each entry (a number against its printed value, a value against the PDF's own text, a row count, the printed name against the person's), dropped because the image is the source and string rules second-guessed the reader's judgement with false marks; reading inside the chat turn (blocks the chat, lost on Stop); one AI call per page (headings and dates lost between pages); one answer at the end (a compaction or a crash loses the work); the chat AI starting readers (every document is read anyway, and the Documents page has no turn); a sandbox folder with a shell (the agent's sandbox can read outside it); a separate finish tool (the end of the turn is the finish). The reason: reading is slow and safe to repeat because it changes nothing in the record, so it belongs in a retryable job, while every decision stays in the chat. The cost: one reading at a time (twelve reports wait their turn), the reader runs only on models whose complete set of tools Rufi has pinned and tested (ADR 0010), and a reading can't be stopped yet.

**17 Oct 2026: no waiting for the account; up to three automatic retries.** A reading no longer waits for the sign-in or a usage limit. When the person is signed out or the sign-in has ended (`not_signed_in`, `sign_in_again`, `plan_use_off` at the token), or the service ends the turn for the sign-in or a usage limit, the job fails at once with that reason, in Rufi's or the service's words, and its notebook is kept; **Retry** continues it. The reader no longer checks the sign-in before a run: the run's token request finds it. Gone: the 30-second sign-in poll, the wait until a limit resets, and the job's `waiting` field. A run that failed for a passing reason (a lost connection, a refused answer, ChatGPT unable to renew the sign-in just now (`temporary` at the token, its words kept as the reason), a start that failed for another reason) goes back to the queue as an automatic retry, up to three, after 30 s, 1 min and 2 min (`jobs.retries`, `jobs.retry_at`, migration 9); after the third, or at Rufi's deadline, the job fails with its reason. The person's Retry starts the count again. Attempt fencing, failed-entry marking and finishing only on a normally completed turn are unchanged. The reason: a reading that waits quietly hides why nothing happens, while a visible reason with Retry is the same as a signed-out send in the chat (ADR 0004). The cost: documents added while signed out or at the limit each need a Retry. TODO: resume them by itself later, with the reason shown in notifications.

**18 Oct 2026: the reader copies; Rufi AI drafts (ADR 0019).** The reader that copies each page (its page copies) is now the only reader Rufi runs. A reading's end no longer makes draft items or decides a kind or date: in the same transaction as its end line, it asks Rufi AI to sort the file, and Rufi AI writes the drafts. A reading where no page could be read asks for nothing. Pages an older reader saved as notebook entries keep them.

**24 Oct 2026: the reading runs in the person's reply, not in the background (ADR 0022).** A document is read when a message sends it, as the first part of the reply (Rufi's part), not when it is added. The reader is a call for one job with an abort signal, from the chat; the loop, the idle poll and `readings.next` are gone, and one slot keeps readings one at a time across Rufi. A job is made when its reply starts and is queued or running only inside it: whatever way Rufi's part ends, it fails the jobs it made that haven't finished, with their copies kept, and each start of Rufi fails any left over ("Rufi closed while reading"). So a reading can be stopped (Stop ends it), and after a restart it doesn't resume by itself: **Retry** on the reply goes on from the next page not copied. A reading that doesn't finish ends the reply before the AI. A reading writes no start or end line in the chat; its Activity step is its record, and it no longer asks for a sort: the same reply hands the message to Rufi AI. The reader's lock-down, its two tools, its model rules (ADR 0010), the notebook-free page copies, the deadline and the three automatic retries are unchanged. The cost named above, "a reading can't be stopped yet", is gone.

**30 Oct 2026: a reading agent can save a whole reading.** Beside the reader, an agent can read a document as the `reading-documents` skill says: it keeps its notes on each page in the AI's folder (`ai/work/<day>/<document id>.md`), splits them by page itself and calls `save_reading` once. In the chat's transaction, Rufi writes what a reader's run writes, through the same helpers: a reading in `jobs`, a copy of each page with its bookkeeping, and its end, so the copies wait to be sorted as the reader's do. Code reads none of the words; it checks only whose document it is, that it wasn't deleted first, and that each page is the document's, once. What Rufi keeps of the call holds no page text, so deleting the document takes its words with it; its notes in the AI's folder go too, and at each start and once a day Rufi removes the AI's days older than 3. The reader, its lock-down and its runtime are unchanged. Considered: saving page by page as the reader does (a page per call, and a reading that ends with no call that finishes it); keeping the notes as the reading (the AI's folder isn't the record, and isn't in backups). The cost: nothing of a reading is in the record until its one save, so a reading agent that stops before it saves leaves only its notes, which go with their day.

**31 Oct 2026: readers are Rufi AI's sub-agents (ADR 0022).** The chat no longer runs the reader: Rufi AI starts reading agents, which save with `save_reading`. A reading saved of fewer pages than the document has takes the pages no reading has copied as its own too, so they show not looked at, never read, and its answer names them. The reader's runner, its lock-down and `/mcp/reader` stay, unused by the chat, until they go.
