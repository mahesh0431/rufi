# 0021 · A Visit keeps the doctor's words; records saved from it cite it

**Date:** 24 Oct 2026 · **Status:** accepted

A visit to a doctor leaves a paper (a prescription, an e-prescription, a discharge summary) or only what the person remembers. Rufi used to keep two unconnected things for it: a `prescriptions` row per item, and a care event of type `visit`. A medicine the person takes couldn't say which visit it came from, and a condition couldn't say which visits it came up in.

One record, the **Visit** (`visits`), is any care event that happened: a clinic, online (a phone call too), home, or a hospital stay (admitted on `date`, discharged on `ended`). It keeps the doctor's words as one Markdown text, as written (`as_written`), with the paper's own headings, the medicines proposed included; beside it, a few fields for lists: setting, dates, doctor, place, and a short reason. A clinic slip, a discharge summary and a visit told in the chat fit the same shape. A visit to come is a follow-up. A prescription stays a kind of document: the paper, not the record.

The records saved from a Visit (each medicine the person takes, a condition, a follow-up) cite it as a source: `record_sources` gains the type `visit`, with `visit_id` under a composite foreign key, so any record can cite any number of Visits and a Visit can be cited by many records. There are no medicine lines inside a Visit and no proposed-or-taking state: a medicine the doctor proposed and the person doesn't take is in the doctor's words with no medicine citing it, and Rufi AI asks. A Visit cites its paper's page; a record from the Visit cites the Visit, not the page again. The person's own facts ("since Monday") keep their message as a source beside it.

`save_changes` gains three things (ADR 0005). `from` names the pages and Visits a change rests on; code checks they are this person's, the document isn't deleted and the Visit isn't removed, never what they say. `action: "link"` adds sources to a record and changes none of its values, for one already in the record (the same medicine, continued). `ref` names a record an add makes, so the Visit and the records from it go in one save, one transaction and one receipt; a ref no earlier change of the save adds refuses the save. A Visit never makes a record by itself: each record from it is its own change, on the person's word. Undo stays the AI's reverse save; latest saves show the pages and Visits each change added or took off, so the reverse can take them off.

The reason: one record keeps what the doctor said whole, and many-to-many sources connect it to what the person does without a second set of medicine lines to keep in step. The judgement (what the paper says, which medicines are taken, a dose that changed) is the AI's with the person, written in the `visits` skill. The cost: what came from a Visit is found by the sources that cite it, not by a list inside it; a draft item saved from a Visit's page cites the page, not the Visit (TODO).
