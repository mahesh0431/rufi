# 0006 · The AI reads one person's record through get_records, by construction

**Date:** 8 Oct 2026, continuation, exact ids and sources added 10 Oct 2026 · **Status:** accepted

Beside `get_overview`, the AI reads the record with one tool, `get_records(kind, from?, to?, ids?, after?)`: the active person's records of one kind (the kinds `save_changes` takes), all of them or those dated between two dates, as stored, with removed records and replaced results marked. The person is the conversation's, chosen by Rufi; the tool takes no person, table or SQL from the AI, and its one query is fixed in code, with the person, the kind's table and the dates bound by Rufi, through the existing read-only connection. So no read can reach another person, the conversations or the settings, whatever the AI sends. Each kind is filtered by one date (a result by when it was measured, a symptom by its onset…); a date known only to the year or month counts when it overlaps the period, and an undated record comes only in a read without dates. At most 200 records and 32 KB of them (as UTF-8 JSON, under what Codex passes on to the model whole) come back, newest first; the description states the same numbers. When a cap leaves records out, the answer says `"complete": false` and gives `next`, where the read stopped (its date, undated or not, and the row), and the AI reads on with `after` set to it until the read is complete, also within one day or past the undated records; Used shows the capped read. With `ids`, the read gets exactly those records (the ones the user added to a message) and names those not found. Each record comes with its version (the save that last changed it) and its sources (the message, or the document and page); a read that fails gives the AI an error, never an empty list. The AI does counts, trends and comparisons itself on the records it read. Considered: one read-only SQL query on a copy of the person's record, in a child process killed at a time limit (it does more in one call, but needs a copy, a process and a kill to stay safe, and the AI can count and compare a person's records itself today); SQL may come back when records grow (documents, M3). The reason: the person's scope is one of the few things that must never go wrong, and a fixed query makes it true by construction, with nothing to bound or stop. The cost: a question over many records takes several reads, and two reads in one reply can see the record before and after a save between them; what each read got back is kept with the conversation, and this race is accepted.
