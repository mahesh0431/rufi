# 0016 · Open work: derived where it has a home, stored where it has none

**Date:** 12 Oct 2026 · **Status:** accepted

Some of what waits for the person lived only in the chat's text. A question the AI asked ("Save HbA1c 6.1 %?") was buried after a few messages, and a new AI thread, seeded with the last 10 messages, didn't know it at all. The AI couldn't see Needs you either.

**Derived, not copied.** Open work is what Rufi helps the person finish or keeps for them to come back to. Documents to save, readings that failed and follow-ups due already have their own data and rules (the document's reading, the due rule); they stay worked out from it each time Needs you or the AI reads them. Nothing copies them into a second store, so they can't disagree.

**Stored, only what has no home.** An open item is a question the AI asked the person, or a task the AI accepted that spans replies; the kind says who acts next. It keeps its text, complete in itself, and refs: the chips of what it is about, checked against the person and stamped with their versions when it was added, so an old question keeps its meaning and its detail can say how each ref stands now (same, changed, removed, deleted, gone). Its states are open, done and dropped; Later is a show-again time on an open item, not a state. `open_items` holds the current state and `open_item_changes` every change in order: who made it (the AI or the person), when, the acting reply for an AI change, and the item after it. Reopen clears the current closing; the history keeps the earlier one.

**One module owns it** (`core/work/open-work.ts`). Overview's Needs you, the person's actions on it, the AI's two tools and the snapshot each turn carries all go through it. It owns what must never go wrong: each person's items are their own (composite foreign keys, and every read filters the person); every change and its history row are one transaction; every change but an add names the version it was made on, and a stale one is refused with the item as it is now; an AI change is keyed by its chat and call, so the same call with the same input returns its first answer and changes nothing, and with other input is refused, and this is looked up before the version, so a lost answer's retry isn't refused by its own change. Rufi resolves the reply from the agent's turn id in the tool's trusted callback; the model never names a person, chat, reply or call. After a commit the module tells its listeners, and the chat's stream tells every open window to read Needs you again; there is no new socket or event bus.

**Not health operations.** A change to open work makes no receipt, raises no record version and is never the person's yes. Closing a question can name the save that answered it; that save went through its own consent and receipt (ADR 0005). Which save answers which question, what "not now" means and when a question is worth recording are the AI's judgement, never code that reads the person's words.

**The AI sees it every turn.** Each turn's input carries a short snapshot labelled as Rufi's current state, never the person's words or consent: the open questions and tasks (at most 10 lines, then a count), how many are on Later, and the counts of the derived rows. It is sent every turn, also "nothing open", so a question dropped on the screen can't live on in the AI's memory, and a fresh thread knows what is open however old. `get_open_work` reads the rest, and one item whole; `track_open_work` makes one change per call.

**A narrow exception for event turns** (ADR 0004). A turn a Rufi event started still can't save, dismiss, retry a reading or make a brief. In it the AI can record a question it asks, and close as done an item a save already answered or its own task. It can't answer, drop, reopen or put off the person's question because of an event.

**Screen actions outside the chat.** Later, Bring back, Drop and Reopen are the person's own actions on Needs you, like a document's delete: they change only open work, never the record, so they don't go through the chat.

**Nothing starts by itself.** Open work never starts a turn. An unfinished task waits; the AI sees it at the next turn and offers to go on. Backups, the export and restore include the tables like any other; a restored item starts nothing and is never a new permission to save. The 7-day and 30-day windows are filters on reads; history is never deleted.

Considered: one stored inbox for everything (a second copy of documents and follow-ups that can drift); a snapshot only when it changed, with a hash per thread (a dropped question would live on in the AI's memory, and delivery would need its own bookkeeping); questions stored as text only (an old "save these?" loses which report it meant); inferring unfinished work from interrupted turns (a stopped turn doesn't say what was accepted). The costs, accepted: work the AI didn't record before a stop isn't recovered; the Needs you read is an honest snapshot, so a change during it shows at the next read; two calls that add the same question are two items, and the AI is told to reuse an open one rather than code de-duplicating text; an item isn't reopened when its save is undone (a TODO).
