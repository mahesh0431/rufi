# 0019 · Rufi AI sorts each read file and drafts its results; the reader only copies

**Date:** 18 Oct 2026, no page check 24 Oct 2026 · **Status:** accepted

Who decides what a page means changes. Before, the reader read each page into an entry (its facts and items), and Rufi's code made the rest: a result's date from the first date on its page when it printed none, results grouped into reports by matching lab, number and date across pages and documents, a document's kind and date guessed from its entries, and the reading's line ("Lab report · 12 Aug · 42 results read", "6 reports · 4 dates"). A file that held several reports, or a mixed pile (reports, a prescription, a letter), stayed one lump, and the guesses could put a result under the wrong report or date, with no one to ask. Now the reader copies each page and decides nothing (its page copies, ADR 0009), and **Rufi AI**, the chat's AI, decides: it splits the file the person added (the add) into documents, gives each its kind, date, lab and report number, and drafts each current result and measurement on its page with the words it came from and, when it is worth a look, why. It sees the person's record and chat, so it can compare with earlier results and ask when it can't tell. Its tips are in the `sorting-documents` skill, as ways to tell, not rules.

**One call, kept whole or not at all.** `sort_documents` takes the whole sort of one add. Each group of pages names the add's document it is by its id (the add's own id for its first document), or names none and is a new document (`documents.from_document`); code doesn't guess which document a group is. A named document gets the fields the call gives, and keeps those it leaves out, so a sort never blanks a lab or date it didn't mean to change. In one transaction (foreign keys checked at its end, so pages and their drafts move together) Rufi updates the named documents, makes the new ones, moves pages (their positions in the add stay), replaces the untouched drafts of the pages named, moves fixed and set-aside drafts with their page at a new version, tombstones a document left without pages, and marks the readings sorted (`jobs.sorted_at`). The call and Rufi's answer are kept in the same transaction: the tool call's row (`tool_calls`, written in the chat's transaction that the sort runs in, as a save writes its receipt) and the request's `answer`, which the same call id gets again. If the call's row can't be kept, nothing of the sort is.

**What code still keeps.** Only what must never go wrong: whose data it is (the person's adds only; a turn Rufi started may sort only its own request's add, only the pages not sorted yet, each into a new document or into one whose kind, date, lab and number it leaves as they are, and change nothing else: no save, set-aside or brief; a face with `show_face` is allowed, as it writes nothing and is no sign of a sort or a save); the add's own document stays (it holds the add's readings); every page of the add in one document, and none not sorted left out; a page a saved result cites stays in its document; and a draft is on a page of the call's documents that has a reader's copy. A refusal names every problem and writes nothing. What a draft says is Rufi AI's reading of the page, kept as given, its `value_number` too: code doesn't compare it with the copy. The reader and Rufi AI read the page, Rufi AI marks what is worth a look, and the person checks the drafts before they save. Rejected: keeping the date fill-in and the report matching for Rufi AI's drafts; a draft's report is its document.

**The one thing Rufi hands the AI without the person's send.** A reading's end writes a request (`sort_requests`, open) and its note as a Rufi row in the chat's queue, in one transaction with its end line. This is an exception to "nothing goes to the AI until the person sends" (ADR 0004): the person added the file, and Rufi asks only for the sort. A sort row goes in a turn of its own, never rides with a message, goes after a person's message sent while it waits to start, and isn't held by the pause after a restart (a hand-over cut off by a restart waits again: `sort_documents` does it once). A Stop holds it until the person's next message, and signed out it waits for the sign-in ("Not sorted yet · Sign in with ChatGPT; Rufi AI sorts it then", with no Retry). A restore ends open requests (nothing from the past runs by itself); they wait for **Retry**.

**24 Oct 2026: no page check.** Code no longer refuses a sort when a draft's value isn't found in its page's copy (Mahesh: "Let's just clean the mess up."). A text search proved little: pages are often images, a value Rufi AI read right from the image failed it, and one mismatch refused the whole sort; it proved only that the characters were somewhere on the page, not that they were the right row's. The `sorting-documents` skill keeps what matters: copy faithfully, look at the page when unsure, and say what is worth a look.

**When it doesn't sort.** The sorter (`server/sorter.ts`) looks after each turn, at start and as retries fall due: a turn that completed without sorting asked the person ("Not sorted yet · Rufi AI asked you about it in the chat"); one the person stopped waits for their next message; a failed turn or hand-over is tried again after 30 seconds, 1 minute and 2 minutes, then waits for **Retry**. Retry on the Documents page sorts first when a sort waits. A newer reading's request of the same add voids the older one. While a file is sorting or waits, it has no **Ask Rufi**.

**Older data.** Migration 12 adds the columns and keeps everything as it was: a reading that had ended counts as sorted, so nothing old is sorted by itself; each older draft kept, in `draft_items.report`, the report and date Rufi's guesses gave it, until migration 13 dropped them (24 Oct 2026): an older draft now saves as Rufi AI's do, with its own date and its document as its report. An older document's pages that a Retry reads again are copied and sorted the new way.

**Deleting a split document** deletes that document only: its pages' copies and drafts go, and a reading stays while another document of the add still has its copies or drafts, and goes with the last of them.

The cost, accepted: a sort is a turn in the person's chat and uses their plan, also when they didn't write; a person's message sent while a sort runs waits for it.
