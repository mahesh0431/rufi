# 0022 · Files start on Send: Rufi copies the pages in the person's reply

**Date:** 24 Oct 2026, its reading by Rufi AI's readers 31 Oct 2026 · **Status:** accepted

Before, a file started work the moment it was chosen: it was uploaded, its reading began in the background, and when the reading ended Rufi started a reply of its own to sort it (ADR 0019). The chip still sat in the box, looking unsent, while Rufi read, wrote lines in the chat and answered; a question the person wrote meanwhile was answered in another reply after the sorting. Now attaching a file from anywhere (the paperclip, a drop on the chat, **Add…** or a drop on the Documents page) only puts its chip in the chat box. Nothing starts before Send. On Send, the files and the words go as one message, and one reply does the work: first **Rufi's part**, where Rufi (not the AI) has its reader copy the pages, one file after another, each an Activity step with its progress; then Rufi AI gets the message and a note of what was copied, sorts the new files with its skill, and answers what the person wrote. The saves still need the person's yes.

**A file is stored when chosen, and sent when sent.** Choosing uploads the file as before (the original in `files/`, a `documents` row and its pages), but the document is unsent: `documents.sent_at` is null. It has no reading job, and it is left out of the Documents page, the AI's tools, the Overview and every count. The send's transaction sets `sent_at` for every unsent document among the message's chips; a document Rufi AI splits from an add takes the add's. Uploading at once keeps the files through a reload or a restart (the chips are already kept in the draft) and keeps the send small. Taking an unsent file's chip out removes it from Rufi in one transaction, with its pages, and its original when nothing else uses it; nothing points at it, so it needs no tombstone (ADR 0015 exists so that what points at a document can say "document deleted"). The removal refuses once `sent_at` is set, so a Send and a × at the same moment never both win.

**A job lives inside a reply.** Reading jobs are made when a reply starts, in the transaction that starts it, for the message's documents that need reading: a new job for a document never read, a failed reading queued again to go on where it stopped, and, for a chip marked **read again**, a new job for the pages a finished reading couldn't read or didn't look at. Whichever way Rufi's part ends, it fails every job it made that hasn't finished, with its copies kept, in the reply's end transaction; and every start of Rufi fails the queued or running jobs left over ("Rufi closed while reading"). So a job is queued or running only inside a live reply, nothing resumes by itself after a restart (as with every message since ADR 0004), and the refusal to sort while a reading of the add is queued or running keeps its meaning. A reading's state is the page states and the job, not a loop's memory.

**The thread opens before the copying; Rufi's part runs outside the lane.** The dispatcher claims the message in the chat's lane and opens the thread, which is quick and keeps today's path when it fails (the message held with **Try again** and **New thread**, not yet in the chat). Only then does one transaction start the reply (its turn row, which has no agent turn yet, so `turns.agent_turn_id` and `thread_id` may be null) and put the person's message in the chat. Reading can take many minutes, so Rufi's part runs outside the lane on its own abort controller, which Stop ends; the lane serialises only short actions. Then it takes the lane again to hand over. The reader (`server/reader.ts`) becomes a call for one job with an abort signal, with one slot so readings stay one at a time across Rufi (ADR 0009); the loop, the idle poll and the reading's chat lines are gone.

**Rufi marks the hand-over before it calls the AI.** The message's row is `handing_over` with `sent_as` empty through Rufi's part, and one transaction sets `sent_as` just before the agent's turn starts. At the end of a reply that didn't reach the AI (Stop, a failure, a restart) the stored row says which case it is: `sent_as` empty means the AI surely never got the message, so the row goes back to waiting with the hold `reply_ended` and the reason, and its reply's **Retry** sends the same message again without showing it twice and goes on from the next page not copied; `sent_as` set means Rufi may have handed it over, and ADR 0004's "not sure" applies, never sent again by itself (since 31 Oct 2026, it counts as sent: ADR 0024). Before, a missing agent turn id was taken as proof, and a crash just after the hand-over could send a message twice.

**A reading that doesn't finish ends the reply before the AI.** The AI never gets half a message. The step ends stopped or failed and the reply's last line says why, how far it got, and **Retry**. Account problems (signed out, a sign-in that ended, a usage limit) and Rufi's deadline end the reading at once; a passing failure keeps its three tries (30 s, 1 min, 2 min), now inside the reply. **Retry** on a document, its page or a page's heading puts the document's chip in the box marked **read again**, an optional field of the chip's pointer, and nothing is written before Send, so a Retry chip taken out leaves nothing behind. Sending a document's chip without the mark doesn't read its unreadable pages again, so "save it" never waits for a page that can't be read. The AI's `retry_reading` tool goes with this.

**A message with files to read never goes into a running reply.** It waits in the queue for a reply of its own, so every file is read before the AI answers about it; a message whose files need no reading goes into a reply the person started, as before (steer). During Rufi's part there is no agent turn to steer into, so what the person sends then waits as an ordinary message.

**What Rufi-started replies are for now.** Only Rufi's notes, such as a deleted document, which can read and not save. `sort_documents` is refused in such a reply at the tool caller, beside the save, set-aside and brief refusals, so the read-only promise holds without the request machinery. This ends the one exception to "nothing goes to the AI until the person sends" in ADR 0004 and ADR 0019: Rufi no longer asks the AI for anything on its own, and `sort_requests`, the sorter with its retries, and the queue's sorting rules go (a sort's trace is its stored tool call, written in the sort's transaction as before).

**Older data.** One migration: open sort requests end and their queue rows are removed, so the file shows **Not sorted yet** with **Ask Rufi**; reading and sorting lines already in a chat stay as they were stored.

Considered and rejected:

- **A workflow engine or state-machine library.** Durable-execution engines (Temporal, Inngest, Restate) and state charts (XState) fit long, branching flows that resume by themselves. Rufi forbids resuming by itself, the pipeline has two fixed steps, and its states already live in `jobs`, `chat_queue` and `turns`; a library would add a second store of state beside the database.
- **Holding the files in the browser until Send.** A reload would lose them, and Send would become a large upload that can fail halfway.
- **A tombstone for an unsent file.** Nothing points at it.
- **The reading as an AI tool.** It would leave the AI's turn waiting many minutes on one call, against Codex's tool timeouts, with no clear Stop. Rufi's part is Rufi's own code, with Stop and progress, and the AI starts with the copies ready.
- **A reply that goes on to the AI after a failed reading.** The AI would answer about files it hasn't seen.

The cost, accepted: a message with files has no AI turn until all its pages are copied, so a long scan means minutes before any answer, and the person sees only Rufi's reader's steps meanwhile. **31 Oct 2026: Rufi AI has the files read; Rufi's part is gone.** A message with files is a row like any other: it steers or queues by the person's setting, and stopping everything holds it like any waiting message. When Rufi hands it over, its note to Rufi AI names the files that need reading, by document id and pages, worked out from the stored page states. Rufi AI decides how to have them read: a reader per file that it starts with `start_agent` and the `reading-documents` skill, or a file sent to a reader at work, on the model and effort it picks, and it sets the reader on the document with `set_reader` (the document keeps only the agent's id; whether it is being read is worked out when shown, from the agent's status and its saves); and it decides about a failed reading, and how much of an upload to sort at once. The `sorting-documents` skill offers these as ways, not rules. A reader saves its copies with `save_reading` (ADR 0009), and its done message wakes Rufi AI to sort (ADR 0026). Gone: Rufi's part, its reading jobs and their failing at a reply's end, the `reply_ended` hold and the queue's `note`, and the reply's **Retry** for readings. Older data: a `reply_ended` row, and a row still handing over with nothing sent, becomes waiting; an older reply keeps its reading steps as stored, and an older reading that stopped is read again whole. Considered: keeping Rufi's part with a reader sub-agent inside it (the AI still waits on code, with nothing of it the AI's call). The cost: no page-by-page progress on the screen, only the reader's line and the document's **Reading**; after a reader stops, nothing reads by itself unless Rufi AI starts one, and a second reader of a document, if Rufi AI starts one anyway, saves a second reading.
