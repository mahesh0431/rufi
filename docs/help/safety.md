# What Rufi's AI will and won't do

Rufi's AI helps you keep and understand your health record. It is not a doctor. Rufi gives it these rules itself, before anything else it is told, and you can't turn them off.

In an emergency, don't wait for Rufi: call your emergency number.

## It can research and propose; you decide with your doctor

The AI can look into your records and results, explain what the evidence says, and propose options, for example that a medicine's dose may be worth reviewing. When you ask whether to start, stop or change a medicine or a dose, or are about to act on something, its answer says to decide it with your doctor or prescriber. Under the chat's box, Rufi always says that AI can make mistakes and that you should check important health decisions with your doctor.

It can be wrong. Check with your doctor before you act.

What you do, and what you keep in your health record, is your decision. If you tell the AI what you decided, such as stopping a medicine, it doesn't argue or refuse.

## In an emergency, it tells you to call for help first

If your message may describe an emergency, such as chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of a stroke, severe bleeding, a possible overdose or thoughts of self-harm, the AI first tells you to call the emergency number now. Only then does it say anything else.

When you chose your region (**Settings** › **Emergency region**), the AI gives that region's number, for example 112 in India. Otherwise it tells you to call your local emergency number, and says that your region isn't set in Rufi. It may also give a number it knows, from the web or its own knowledge; then it says where the number comes from. Choose your region, so that the AI gives your region's number.

## It reads numbers carefully

The AI connects different parts of your record, for example your sleep and your glucose: that is what it is for. When it works with numbers, it:

- keeps one test in one unit in a trend,
- treats a missing day as missing, not as zero,
- keeps a value such as "<5" as printed, never as 5,
- says that two things happen together, not that one causes the other,
- says first when the lab flagged a result, such as High, with the lab's range, and only then gives context, such as a common treatment target.

Anything that changes what an answer means, such as only two readings, a gap or a value from the web, is in the answer itself. Under each answer, **Based on** shows where it comes from. See [Where an answer comes from](chat.md#where-an-answer-comes-from).

## It won't help anyone get hurt

The AI doesn't help anyone harm themselves or someone else, for example with doses or methods, however the question is asked. It says it can't help with that and points to the emergency number or a doctor.

## It asks before it saves, and says "saved" only when Rufi saved it

The AI says what it will save and asks you first. It saves only after you say yes, and only what you said about yourself: nothing from a question, an "if" or someone else's news. It says that something was saved only when Rufi confirmed it, and Rufi shows its own receipt for each save. See [Changing your health record](chat.md#changing-your-health-record).
