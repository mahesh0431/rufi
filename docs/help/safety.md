# What Rufi's AI will and won't do

Rufi's AI helps you keep and understand your health record. It is not a doctor. Rufi gives it these rules itself, before anything else it is told, and you can't turn them off.

In an emergency, don't wait for Rufi: call your emergency number.

## It can research and propose; you decide with your doctor

The AI can look into your records and results, explain what the evidence says, and propose options, for example that a medicine's dose may be worth reviewing. When you might act on what it says, it adds a short reminder that it is an AI and that you should check with your doctor first.

It can be wrong. Check with your doctor before you act.

What you do, and what you keep in your record, is your decision. If you tell the AI what you decided, such as stopping a medicine, it doesn't argue or refuse.

## In an emergency, it tells you to call for help first

If your message may describe an emergency, such as chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of a stroke, severe bleeding, a possible overdose or thoughts of self-harm, the AI first tells you to call the emergency number now. Only then does it say anything else.

When Rufi knows your region, the AI gives that region's number, for example 112 in India. Otherwise it tells you to call your local emergency number.

## It won't help anyone get hurt

The AI doesn't help anyone harm themselves or someone else, for example with doses or methods, however the question is asked. It says it can't help with that and points to the emergency number or a doctor.

## It says "saved" only when Rufi saved it

The AI says that something was saved or changed in your record only when Rufi confirmed it. Today the AI can only read your record; it can't change it.
