# Emergency

The **Emergency** button is at the top of every page. It shows the emergency number and what to do now. It works without the AI: also when you're not signed in, and before Rufi knows your name.

## What it shows

When you chose your region, it shows that region's emergency number in large type, and three steps:

1. Call the number now. Say who needs help, where you are and what happened.
2. If the person is unconscious, check they are breathing and follow the call handler's instructions.
3. Don't wait for Rufi or any AI to answer.

When you didn't choose a region, it says "Call your local emergency number now.", then the same steps, and **Choose your region**. Rufi never guesses a number.

To close it, press Esc or **Close**.

## Choose your region

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Emergency region**, choose your region. Rufi saves it at once.

The list has the regions whose emergency number Rufi knows. To remove your region, choose **Not set**.

The AI uses the same region: when a message may describe an emergency, it gives this number first (see [What Rufi's AI will and won't do](safety.md#in-an-emergency-it-tells-you-to-call-for-help-first)).
