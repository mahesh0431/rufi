# Visit brief

Before a doctor's visit, you can ask Rufi for a visit brief: a dated page with your current medicines, recent lab results and measurements, conditions and allergies, and questions to ask. Every fact in it comes from your saved health record, and Rufi lists those records under the brief. You can copy it and paste it wherever you need it.

## Ask for a brief

In the chat, ask for it in your own words, for example "Make a brief for my visit with Dr Mehra on Friday". You can say what the visit is about ("mostly about my sugar"). When the AI can't tell what the visit is for and that changes what goes in, it asks you first.

The AI reads your record, writes the brief and saves it. The chat then shows Rufi's line for it, with a page sign and the day it was made:

> Visit brief · Made 10 Oct 2026 · 9 records cited · **Open**

Rufi writes this line, not the AI. Click **Open** to see the brief.

## What a brief has

The AI picks what matters for this visit. A brief usually has:

- **Current medicines**, with their strength and when you take them.
- **Recent lab results**, those the lab marked outside its range first. Each has its value, unit, lab range and date, as the lab printed them.
- **Recent measurements**, such as blood pressure or weight, when you have any.
- **Conditions and allergies.** When your record has none, the brief says "none recorded". That doesn't mean you have none.
- **Questions to ask**, each starting with "Question:".

A brief uses only what is saved in your record. A document that Rufi read but that you haven't saved yet isn't in it. The AI gives no advice that your record doesn't support.

## The brief's page

The page shows when the brief was made, the brief as the AI wrote it, and **Sources**: each record the brief rests on, as it was when the brief was made, with where that record came from. For example:

| Kind         | Record                     | From                                                                 |
| ------------ | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Medicine use | Magnesium 200 mg · current | Your message, 7 Oct 2026: "I started magnesium…"                     |
| Result       | HbA1c 6.8 % · 12 Aug 2026  | Sunrise Labs report, collected 12 Aug 2026 · Sunrise Labs August.pdf |

Rufi builds this list itself, from the records the AI cited. A record that Rufi had before it kept sources says **Saved before Rufi kept sources**. A page of a document you deleted says **Document deleted (Sunrise Labs August.pdf, page 2)**: the brief itself doesn't change.

## Copy a brief

Click **Copy**. Rufi puts the brief on the clipboard as plain text: the date, the brief and its Sources. **Copied** shows for a moment. Paste it into an email, a note or a message to your doctor.

## When your record changes

A brief never changes after it is made. When you save something to your record after that, the brief's page says **The record changed since this brief was made**, and each source that changed is marked **changed since**. The brief still shows what it said then. To include the changes, ask Rufi for a new brief.

If you save something while the AI is still writing a brief, the brief may not include it. Its page then says that the record changed since.

## Find a brief again

Each brief stays in the chat, at its line. Scroll up to it and click **Open**.
