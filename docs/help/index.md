# Rufi help

Rufi is in early development and not ready for use yet. These pages describe what works today.

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): start Rufi, open it again, and what you see.
2. [Talking to Rufi](chat.md): the chat bar, panel and page; stopping a reply, waiting messages, steering, the model and drafts.
3. [Signing in with ChatGPT](signing-in.md): connect Rufi to your ChatGPT plan, and what Rufi keeps.
4. [Your data and the AI](your-data-and-the-ai.md): where your records are, what the AI sends, what Rufi keeps of your conversations, and what Rufi logs.
5. [What Rufi's AI will and won't do](safety.md): the rules Rufi gives its AI, which you can rely on.

The words Rufi uses are in the [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).
