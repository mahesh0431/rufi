# Rufi help

Rufi is in early development and not ready for use yet. These pages describe what works today.

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): start Rufi, open it as an app, open it again, the first run, and what you see.
2. [Your record](record.md): Overview with Needs you, your record as tiles and recent changes; a page for each kind of record; a record's sources, history and values over time; Ask Rufi.
3. [Talking to Rufi](chat.md): your one chat with Rufi as the bar, panel and page; reading back; asking about your record and where an answer comes from; changing your record and undo; stopping a reply, steering, waiting messages, Rufi's lines, the model, the AI's memory and drafts.
4. [Signing in with ChatGPT](signing-in.md): connect Rufi to your ChatGPT plan, in Rufi or in the terminal, and what Rufi keeps.
5. [Documents](documents.md): add lab reports and other documents as PDFs or photos, how Rufi reads them, and look at the originals.
6. [Follow-ups](follow-ups.md): save something to come back to, when it is due, the Follow-ups page, and the notices on your Mac.
7. [Visit brief](visit-brief.md): ask for a brief before a doctor's visit, what it has, its sources, and copying it.
8. [Your data and the AI](your-data-and-the-ai.md): where your records are, what the AI sends, what Rufi keeps of your chat, and what Rufi logs.
9. [Backups and export](backups-and-export.md): Rufi's backups with your documents, how to restore one, and how to take all your data out.
10. [What Rufi's AI will and won't do](safety.md): the rules Rufi gives its AI, which you can rely on.

The words Rufi uses are in the [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).
